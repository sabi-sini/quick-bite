Who we are

Bringing your favourite restaurants to your door

QuickBite is an online food ordering platform that connects hungry customers with the best restaurants in town — from South Indian tiffin and North Indian curries to pizzas, Chinese, desserts and fresh juices.

We started with a simple idea: ordering food should be easy, the food should arrive hot, and the experience should feel as good as the meal.

Our Mission

To make delicious, freshly prepared food accessible to everyone, while supporting the local restaurants and kitchens that make our city taste great.
